Income

Budgets are calculated from your expected income, so they don't shrink while you're waiting for your second payment. If you log more than expected (e.g. a bonus), the extra is included. USD purchases and payments are converted at this rate.

Category Budgets

Type a % or a ₡ amount - the app fills in the other. A highlighted field is the one you set: a % budget grows or shrinks with your income, and a ₡ amount stays fixed (good for rent). To make room for card debt, every budget without a 🔒 shrinks by the same % (shown in orange); 🔒 budgets are never reduced.

Bank statements

Upload the monthly statement PDF (estado de cuenta) from your bank. It is the bank's own record, so it shows what the emails can't: interest, insurance charges, the amount to pay in full, the due date and your real interest rate. If the bank changes the layout, a statement that doesn't add up is flagged for review instead of being trusted.

Enter what you owe from your bank's app or statement. Colones and dollars are separate balances, each paid in its own currency.
